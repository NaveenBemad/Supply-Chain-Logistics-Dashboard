# Supply Chain & Logistics Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
An end-to-end Freight & Logistics Performance Analysis project focused on evaluating revenue, delivery reliability, fleet costs, and operational efficiency.

The project uses Excel, MySQL, and Power BI to transform logistics data into actionable business insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Excel:** Initial data inspection
- **MySQL:** Data analysis using SQL queries
- **Power BI:** Data transformation, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard development
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation

## Business Objectives
- Analyze revenue performance and trends.
- Evaluate on-time delivery performance and identify high-delay routes.
- Monitor fuel and maintenance expenses.
- Assess fleet utilization and operational efficiency.
- Identify opportunities to improve logistics performance.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Revenue Performance
Revenue analysis and key business performance indicators.

### 2. Delivery Reliability
On-time delivery performance, route-level delays, and delivery delay patterns.

### 3. Fleet Cost & Efficiency
Fuel expenses, maintenance costs, fuel efficiency, and operational cost distribution.

## Key Findings
- On-time delivery performance remained approximately 33% during 2022–2024.
- Route-level late delivery rates exceeded 64%, indicating widespread delivery reliability challenges.
- Average delivery delay remained approximately 2.5 hours across distance groups, suggesting that distance alone does not explain delay patterns.
